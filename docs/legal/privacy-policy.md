## 1. About Us

"We," "our," or "us" refers to OfflineGameNia. "You" or "user" refers to anyone using our website or services. This Policy applies to personal information processed in connection with browsing our site, creating an account, purchasing Products or Subscriptions, Activating Licenses, using My Games or Steam Guard features, and contacting support.

This Policy does not apply to third-party Platforms (Steam, Epic Games, Microsoft, Discord, payment networks, etc.) that process data under their own policies when you use their services.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the Service:

- **Account information** — email address, first and last name (if provided), and Clerk user ID.
- **Purchase information** — buyer email, Order and License identifiers, amounts, currency, Stripe payment identifiers, Subscription IDs, and License status.
- **Technical and security information** — IP address, browser/User-Agent, timestamps, and metadata for security-sensitive actions such as checkout, Activation, Steam Guard requests, admin actions, and session events.
- **Support information** — messages you send to support, and Discord username/content if you use our Discord support channels.

We do not intentionally collect government IDs, precise location, biometric data, or other sensitive personal data as part of standard checkout.

**Payment card data.** Card numbers, CVC, and full payment credentials are processed securely by **Stripe** on Stripe Checkout pages. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

**Shared Account credentials.** Credentials for Shared Accounts (username, encrypted password, 2FA shared secrets) are stored to fulfill Products. They belong to our service infrastructure and are protected with encryption at rest.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

1. Provide, maintain, and secure the Service (accounts, checkout, Licenses, Activation, My Games, Subscriptions);
2. Process payments and prevent fraud;
3. Provide customer support and handle replacements;
4. Investigate incidents, enforce our Terms, and prevent abuse;
5. Operate and improve the Service; and
6. Comply with legal obligations and defend legal claims.

We do **not** sell your personal information for money.

## 4. Third-Party Processors

We rely on carefully selected service providers to run the Service:

- **Clerk** — authentication, sessions, and user identity.
- **Stripe** — payment processing, Subscriptions, and refunds.
- **Neon (PostgreSQL)** — primary application database.
- **Discord** — community and support channels.
- **Google Gemini (or similar AI)** — support reply suggestions, if used.
- **Sentry** — error monitoring, if enabled.
- **Hosting providers** (e.g., Vercel / Railway) — application hosting and transport.

Each provider has its own privacy policy. We configure them to process only what is needed.

## 5. Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage required for the Service to function, including Clerk authentication/session cookies and hosting/security cookies. As of the Last updated date, we do not operate first-party analytics. If we add non-essential cookies later, we will update this Policy and obtain consent where required.

When you are redirected to Stripe Checkout, Stripe may set cookies subject to Stripe's policies.

## 6. Data Sharing

We share personal information only:

1. With the service providers listed above, under contracts or terms requiring appropriate protection;
2. With professional advisors (lawyers, accountants) under confidentiality where needed;
3. With authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and security; and
4. In connection with a business transfer (merger, sale, or restructure), subject to notice or this Policy.

## 7. Data Storage & Security

Your personal data is stored on secure servers hosted by our trusted partners. We implement technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including HTTPS in transit, encryption at rest for Shared Account credentials, access controls, role-based permissions, authentication via Clerk, payment handling by Stripe, rate limiting on sensitive endpoints, and audit logging.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for safeguarding your OfflineGameNia account credentials.

## 8. International Transfers

OfflineGameNia and some processors may process data in the United States, the European Union, and other countries. Where personal data is transferred from the EEA/UK to countries without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent mechanisms provided by our processors.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described, including:

| Data | Typical retention |
|------|-------------------|
| Account profile | Life of the account, then deleted or anonymized after deletion request |
| Orders and Licenses | License Term plus period for warranty, disputes, accounting, and legal retention |
| Subscription records | Subscription life plus legal/accounting retention |
| Audit logs | Limited security period unless needed longer for investigations |
| Support tickets | As long as needed to resolve issues, then archived or deleted |
| Stripe records | Retained by Stripe per its policies; we keep identifiers needed for reconciliation |

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it, except where continued retention is required by law or dispute resolution.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, withdraw consent where processing is consent-based, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, email **support@offlinegamenia.com** with the subject line "Privacy Request" and include enough information for us to verify your identity. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (generally 30 days under GDPR, subject to extensions for complex requests). Some requests may be denied or limited where an exemption applies.

---

## 11. California Residents

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale/sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration or knowingly share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise California rights, contact **support@offlinegamenia.com**. You may use an authorized agent subject to verification.

---

## 12. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 (or under the digital age of consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

## 13. Account Deletion

Where available, you may delete your account from account settings. This typically removes your OfflineGameNia user profile and the corresponding Clerk authentication user. Deleting your account may result in loss of access to Licenses and purchase history, and does not by itself entitle you to a refund.

We may retain limited records after deletion where needed for legal obligations, fraud prevention, accounting, or dispute resolution.
