## 1. Overview

OfflineGameNia sells **digital access Licenses** to **Shared Accounts** on Platforms such as Steam, Epic Games Store, and Microsoft Store / Xbox. These are digital goods delivered electronically.

Because digital content can be accessed immediately:

- Sales are generally **final after Delivery**, except as stated below or required by mandatory consumer law;
- Change of mind after Delivery is typically **not** refundable;
- Where a Product is defective or Delivery fails, we provide **replacement** and/or **refund** as described below.

---

## 2. What Counts as Delivery

**Delivery** occurs at the earlier of:

1. Your **License Key** being issued or displayed (on the checkout success page, in My Games, or otherwise made available to you); or
2. **Activation** — you Activate the License and Shared Account credentials are made available to you.

Once Delivery has occurred, the Product is considered supplied as digital content. Save your License Key immediately after purchase. Incorrect buyer email at checkout can make recovery harder and does not by itself create a refund right.

---

## 3. Refund Window

You are eligible for a **full refund** within **14 days** of purchase if:

- Your License has **not** been Activated;
- Shared Account credentials have **not** been accessed or viewed; and
- You have not otherwise used the entitlement beyond displaying the License Key for safekeeping.

If you have Activated or retrieved credentials, this 14-day window does not apply, but you may still be eligible under the defective-access rules below.

You are also eligible for a full refund if:

- We charged you but failed to deliver a License Key within a reasonable time after successful payment; or
- You were charged twice for the same Order or charged due to a clear billing error attributable to us or Stripe.

---

## 4. Defective or Broken Access

If, after purchase, Shared Account credentials are **invalid, previously compromised, or otherwise unusable** due to a defect on our side, and we cannot provide a working replacement within a reasonable time, you may receive a **full refund** or replacement at our reasonable discretion.

For eligible one-time Licenses during the marketed two-year access period, we will use commercially reasonable efforts to **fix the issue free of charge** or assign a **replacement Shared Account**. Replacement is the primary remedy for access issues; a refund is typically provided only if replacement is not reasonably possible, mandatory law requires it, or we agree a refund is fair after investigation.

We may ask for screenshots, error messages, License Key, and purchase email to verify defects.

---

## 5. Not Eligible for Refund

Unless required by mandatory law, refunds are **not** available for:

- Change of mind after Delivery;
- Hardware, OS, or driver incompatibility;
- Ignoring Product "Important Information" such as offline/single-player limits, shared-account nature, cloud-gaming incompatibility, or non-transferability;
- Acceptable Use violations, including modifying Shared Account credentials, attempting takeover, reselling or publicly sharing credentials, or chargeback abuse;
- Platform bans or restrictions caused by your conduct, or Platform outages;
- Region or Platform policy conflicts where shared-account access is unsuitable;
- Consumed Subscription time after a billing period has started;
- Lost License Keys or incorrect buyer email after Delivery;
- Purchases made through unauthorized third parties.

## 6. Subscriptions

You may cancel a Subscription anytime to stop future renewals. Access typically continues until the end of the paid period.

Fees for a billing period that has started are generally non-refundable, except where required by law, we charged in error, or we fail to provide Subscription benefits through no fault of yours and cannot cure within a reasonable time. We do not generally offer pro-rata refunds for mid-period cancellations unless required by law.

## 7. EU / UK Statutory Rights

If you are a consumer in the **EU**, **UK**, or a similar jurisdiction, you normally have up to **14 days** to withdraw from a distance purchase without giving a reason.

Our Products are digital content supplied immediately on request. By completing checkout and requesting immediate Delivery, you expressly request that we begin supply before the withdrawal period expires and acknowledge that you **lose the right of withdrawal** once Delivery begins, to the extent permitted by applicable law. This waiver does **not** remove your statutory rights if digital content is faulty or not as described.

When implemented, we recommend an explicit checkout checkbox confirming immediate Delivery and loss of withdrawal rights after supply begins.

## 8. How to Request a Refund

Email **support@offlinegamenia.com** (preferred), use the support form, or escalate via Discord for access emergencies. Never post credentials in public Discord.

Please include:

- Purchase email address;
- Order / Stripe receipt identifier, if available;
- License Key;
- Game title and Platform;
- Whether you Activated or viewed credentials;
- Description of the issue and screenshots/error messages;
- Preferred remedy (refund or replacement).

We may review Activation logs, Order status, audit events, and Shared Account health to determine eligibility. Submitting false information may result in denial and account action.

---

## 9. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method via Stripe** whenever possible. Banks and card networks typically complete posting within **5–10 business days**. When a refund is approved, related Licenses may be revoked and Shared Account seats released. Refunds are processed in the charged currency (USD unless otherwise stated); FX differences charged by your bank are outside our control.

We aim to acknowledge requests within a few business days and resolve most cases within **14 days** of receiving complete information. Complex Platform-side issues may take longer.

---

## 10. Chargebacks

Contact us first before opening a chargeback. Chargebacks opened without contacting support often delay good-faith resolution and may look like fraud.

If you file a chargeback after Delivery of digital content, we may immediately revoke Licenses, suspend your account, contest the chargeback using Delivery records, and ban repeat or abusive chargebacks. Valid disputes (e.g., unauthorized card use, fulfillment failure) will be investigated promptly.

---

## 11. Abuse

We may deny refunds, refuse future sales, and terminate accounts if we reasonably determine that a User is abusing this Policy or our systems, including pattern refunds, Activation-then-chargeback schemes, credential scraping, or multi-accounting to evade limits.
