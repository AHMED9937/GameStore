## 1. Overview

OfflineGameNia sells **digital access Licenses** to **Shared Accounts** (Steam, Epic Games, Microsoft Store / Xbox, and other Platforms as listed). These are digital goods delivered electronically.

Because digital content can be used, copied, or accessed immediately:

- Sales are generally **final after Delivery**, except as stated in this Policy or required by mandatory consumer law;
- Change of mind after Delivery is typically **not** refundable;
- Where a Product is defective or Delivery fails, we provide **replacement** and/or **refund** as described below.

Industry practice for digital key marketplaces (for example Eneba-style rules: refund before reveal; refund if invalid) informs this Policy. OfflineGameNia is a **direct seller**, not a multi-seller marketplace, so one set of rules applies to all purchases on our Service.

## 2. What Counts as Delivery

**Delivery** occurs at the **earlier** of:

1. **License Key issued or displayed** — for example on the checkout success page, in My Games, or otherwise made available to you; or
2. **Activation** — you Activate the License and Shared Account credentials (username/password and, where applicable, Steam Guard codes) are made available to you.

Once Delivery has occurred, the Product is considered supplied as digital content.

**Guest tip:** Save your License Key immediately after purchase. Incorrect buyer email at guest checkout can make recovery harder and does not by itself create a change-of-mind refund right.

## 3. Eligible Refunds

Subject to verification, you are eligible for a **full refund** of the purchase price in the following cases:

### 3.1 Within 14 days — not Activated / credentials not accessed

You may request a refund within **fourteen (14) days** of purchase if:

- Your License has **not** been Activated; and
- Shared Account credentials have **not** been accessed or viewed through the Service; and
- For License-key purposes, you have not otherwise used the entitlement beyond Display of the key solely for safekeeping (we treat Activation / credential access as the primary use threshold).

If you Activated or retrieved credentials, Section 3.1 does not apply (see Sections 4–5).

### 3.2 Payment taken but License not delivered (fulfillment failure)

If Stripe (or our systems) charged you but we failed to issue a License Key within a reasonable time after successful payment (for example Order stuck, webhook failure without eventual fulfillment), contact us. You are entitled to a **full refund** or completion of Delivery at your choice, unless we successfully fulfill promptly after investigation.

### 3.3 Duplicate or erroneous charges

If you were charged twice for the same Order, or charged due to a clear billing error attributable to us or Stripe configuration, you are entitled to a refund of the duplicate/erroneous amount.

### 3.4 Defective or invalid access confirmed by support

If, after purchase (including after Activation), Shared Account credentials are **invalid, previously compromised in a way that prevents play, or otherwise unusable** due to a defect on our side, and we cannot provide a working replacement within a reasonable time, you may receive a **full refund** or a replacement under Section 4, at our reasonable discretion (refund required where mandated by law).

We may ask for screenshots, error messages, License Key, and purchase email to verify defects.

### 3.5 Mandatory consumer rights

Nothing in this Policy limits non-waivable statutory rights in your jurisdiction (including rights relating to faulty digital content).

## 4. Replacement Instead of Refund (Two-Year Access Guarantee)

### 4.1 What we promise

For eligible **one-time** Licenses during the marketed **two-year access period**, if you experience access or connection issues attributable to our Shared Accounts—such as:

- Shared Account login failures;
- Credential rotation on our side that breaks your access;
- Steam Guard / 2FA delivery failure on our systems;
- Shared Account suspension or ban that is not caused by your Acceptable Use violation;

we will use commercially reasonable efforts to **fix the issue free of charge** or assign a **replacement Shared Account**.

### 4.2 Replacement is the primary remedy

Replacement (or repair of access) is the **primary remedy** for access issues. A refund is typically provided only if:

- Replacement is not reasonably possible; or
- Mandatory law requires a refund; or
- We agree a refund is fair after investigation.

### 4.3 Timing and catalog caveats

- Replacement is **not always instantaneous**. High-demand titles are often prioritized during Platform-wide incidents.
- Some games may take a long time to replace; in rare cases a title may be delayed or remaining unavailable for extended periods.
- During such situations, Subscription catalogs may be temporarily shortened, and "Games to Replace" status may appear. **No action is required from you** beyond patience while we restore access where possible.

These operational realities do not change your rights for confirmed defective Products under Section 3.4 or applicable law.

### 4.4 Warranty vs change of mind

The two-year access guarantee is **not** a money-back guarantee if you no longer want the game, dislike gameplay, or find your PC does not meet requirements.

## 5. Not Eligible for Refund

Unless required by mandatory law, refunds are **not** available for:

5.1. **Change of mind after Delivery** — including after License Key display where you later Activate, or after Activation / credential access.

5.2. **Hardware / OS / driver incompatibility** — you are responsible for meeting game system requirements and Platform client requirements.

5.3. **Ignoring Product "Important Information"** — including Steam offline / single-player limits, shared-account nature, cloud-gaming incompatibility, and non-transferability.

5.4. **Acceptable Use violations** — including modifying Shared Account email/password, attempting takeover, reselling credentials, public sharing of credentials, or chargeback abuse.

5.5. **Platform actions unrelated to your individual License quality where you caused the issue** — or bans/restrictions caused by your conduct.

5.6. **Publisher / Platform outages** — temporary downtime of Steam, Epic, Microsoft, or game servers.

5.7. **Region or Platform policy conflicts** — where your local law or a Platform's rules make shared-account access unsuitable; you remain responsible for compliance.

5.8. **Consumed Subscription time** — the current paid billing period after renewal has started (see Section 6).

5.9. **Buyer error with email or lost keys after Delivery** — we will attempt recovery in good faith, but lost credentials alone do not entitle a refund after Delivery.

5.10. **Purchases made through unauthorized third parties** — this Policy covers purchases completed on OfflineGameNia via our Stripe checkout only.

## 6. Subscriptions

6.1. **Cancel anytime.** You may cancel a Subscription to stop future renewals. Access typically continues until the end of the period already paid.

6.2. **Current period.** Fees for a billing period that has started are generally **non-refundable**, except where:

- Required by mandatory law;
- We charged in error;
- We fail to provide the Subscription benefits through no fault of yours and cannot cure within a reasonable time.

6.3. **Pro-rata refunds.** We do not generally offer pro-rata refunds for mid-period cancellations, unless required by law or granted as a discretionary goodwill gesture.

6.4. **Catalog changes.** Included games may change. Temporary catalog reductions for operational reasons (for example account replacement campaigns) are not automatically refundable if remaining access has material value; contact us if you believe a material service failure occurred.

## 7. EU / UK Statutory Withdrawal Rights

### 7.1 Right of withdrawal for distance contracts

If you are a consumer in the **European Union**, **United Kingdom**, or another jurisdiction with a similar distance-selling withdrawal right, you may normally have up to **fourteen (14) days** to withdraw from a distance purchase without giving a reason.

### 7.2 Immediate digital supply — waiver upon Delivery

OfflineGameNia Products are digital content supplied immediately on request. By completing checkout and requesting immediate Delivery, you:

1. **Expressly request** that we begin supply of digital content before the withdrawal period expires; and
2. **Acknowledge** that you **lose the right of withdrawal** once Delivery begins (License Key issued/displayed or Activation / credential access — whichever is earlier), to the extent permitted by Directive 2011/83/EU (and UK Consumer Contracts Regulations) and equivalent laws.

### 7.3 Faulty digital content

The waiver above does **not** remove your statutory rights if digital content is not in conformity with the contract (faulty/defective) under applicable consumer law.

### 7.4 Checkout disclosure

Checkout currently states that purchases grant a License for shared-account access and that refunds follow store policy. When implemented, we recommend an explicit checkbox (see Section 13) confirming immediate Delivery and loss of withdrawal rights after supply begins.

## 10. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

10.1. **Contact us first.** Chargebacks open without contacting support often delay good-faith resolution and may look like fraud.

10.2. If you file a chargeback after Delivery of digital content:

- We may **immediately revoke** Licenses and suspend your account;
- We may contest the chargeback with Stripe and your bank using Delivery records (Order completion, License issuance, Activation logs);
- Repeat or abusive chargebacks may result in a permanent ban.

10.3. Valid disputes (for example unauthorized card use, fulfillment failure) will be investigated promptly. Unauthorized card use should also be reported to your bank.
